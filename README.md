Multi-tenant platform serving two Costa Rican brands — a tech equipment retailer and a natural products store — from a single database, each with its own storefront, catalog, and per-brand theming. Includes AI-automated quote generation, a support chatbot, and Google OAuth2 authentication.
* **Tech Stack:** `Next.js` `React` `TypeScript` `RTK Query` · `Express` `Prisma` `PostgreSQL` `Redis`

Production raffle platform for Lions Beach Home. Timed number reservations prevent double-booking, a public results verifier lets anyone independently confirm a draw, a full audit trail tracks every reservation, and automated WhatsApp/Telegram/email notifications keep participants informed.
* **Tech Stack:** `Next.js` `React 19` `TypeScript` `Redux Toolkit` · `Express` `Prisma` `PostgreSQL`

A Jenga tower where each block has a math problem you have to solve to place it. Built for fun, kept as a showcase: colorblind mode, full keyboard navigation, a lives system, and bilingual (English/Spanish) support.
